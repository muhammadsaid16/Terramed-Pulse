## 🌍 Executive Summary (الموجز التنفيذي)

**English:**  
Egypt stands at a critical climate and human capital crossroads. While global analyses often treat national climate warming as a uniform phenomenon, 31 years of NASA satellite Earth observations (1995–2025) reveal **starkly opposing regional trends** across Egypt’s geographic coordinates:
- In **Upper Egypt (Aswan, Assiut)**, mean air temperature ($T_{2M}$) rises steadily at $+0.35^\circ\text{C}$/decade ($p < 0.001$, Mann-Kendall $Z = 3.59$), with daily maximum temperatures ($T_{2M\_MAX}$) increasing at $+0.49^\circ\text{C}$/decade in Aswan, driving a cumulative $+1.46^\circ\text{C}$ peak temperature increase over the 30-year record alongside severe topsoil desiccation (GLDAS Soil Moisture dropping by $-1.52\,\text{kg/m}^2$/decade, $p < 0.0001$). Radiometric land surface temperature ($LST_{DAY}$ from MODIS) escalates at $+1.41^\circ\text{C}$ to $+1.44^\circ\text{C}$/decade.
- In **Greater Cairo & the Nile Delta (Cairo, Giza, Alexandria)**, fine particulate pollution (MERRA-2 $PM_{2.5}$) escalates at $+7.05\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$/decade ($p < 0.0001$, baseline $> 78\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$) alongside agricultural greenness loss (MODIS NDVI $-0.022$/decade, $p < 0.0001$) and heavy soil moisture depletion ($-2.06\,\text{kg/m}^2$/decade in Alexandria, $p < 0.0001$). Meanwhile, maritime buffering in Alexandria keeps daily maximum warming lower ($+0.16^\circ\text{C}$/decade).

Compounding this environmental divergence is Egypt's severe physician brain drain: over 50% of recent medical graduates seek opportunities abroad (WHO EMHJ cohort, $n=885$), leaving Upper Egypt with as few as **5.2 physicians per 10,000 residents** (compared to 16.5 in Cairo, CAPMAS 2024).

**Terramed Pulse ("Stay & Build Egypt" — ابقَ وابنِ)** bridges this gap. It acts as an interactive Earth System Trend Detective and public health compass that connects NASA satellite data directly to clinical demand, crowdsources verified physician field observations, and equips decision-makers with a "What-If" policy simulation lab to retain medical talent and protect vulnerable communities.

## 🔬 Scientific Triangulation: Delineating NASA Data, Potential Health Impact & Medical Statistics

To maintain strict scientific integrity and avoid conflating remote sensing observations with clinical epidemiology, Terramed Pulse clearly differentiates between three distinct evidence tiers:

| Evidence Tier | Data Source & Nature | What It Proves (Strengths) | Methodological Boundary (Limitations) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Environmental Evidence**<br>*(القرائن البيئية)* | **NASA Earth Observations:**<br>• NASA POWER ($T_{2M}, T_{2M\_MAX}, RH_{2M}$)<br>• NASA MERRA-2 ($PM_{2.5}, AOD$)<br>• NASA MODIS Terra/Aqua ($NDVI, LST$)<br>• NASA/NOAA GLDAS-2.1 (Soil Moisture, $ET$) | Objective, continuous physical changes in atmospheric and land parameters over 31 years (1995–2025) with rigorous parametric (OLS regression, 95% CI) and non-parametric (Mann-Kendall test, $p < 0.001$) validation. | Measures physical photons, radiance, and atmospheric concentrations. **Does not measure patient admissions, morbidities, or clinical outcomes.** |
| **2. Potential Health Impact**<br>*(الأثر الصحي المحتمل والميكانيزم الحيوي)* | **Biomedical & Pathophysiological Models:**<br>Peer-reviewed medical literature on thermal and toxicological stress pathways: heatstroke, hemoconcentration, acute tubular necrosis, oxidative alveolar airway injury. | Maps physical hazards to organ-specific vulnerabilities across 8 specialties (e.g., extreme heat $\to$ Nephrology/Emergency; aerosols $\to$ Pulmonology), establishing clinical demand urgency. | Represents **exposure-driven vulnerability and plausible epidemiological risk**, not direct clinical diagnostic proof. |
| **3. Actual Medical Statistics**<br>*(الإحصاءات والبيانات الطبية الواقعية)* | **Ground-Truth Healthcare Registries:**<br>• **CAPMAS 2024:** Official physician densities (5.2/10k in Aswan vs 16.5/10k in Cairo) and hospital bed counts.<br>• **WHO EMHJ 2021 ($n=885$):** Documented physician migration drivers and working hours.<br>• **Physician Voice In-Page Observatory:** Local clinical severity scores (1–5) and facility burdens logged by frontline clinicians. | Real-world clinical capacity, systemic healthcare workforce shortages, and actual field-reported case burdens in Egyptian governorates. | Reflects healthcare institutional capacity and workforce retention; does not track satellite physics directly. |

## 📐 Mathematical & Statistical Architecture

Terramed Pulse features an in-browser statistical calculation engine that executes non-parametric and parametric trend tests on demand with zero server roundtrips:

### 1. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression & Student's t 95% Confidence Interval
$$\text{Slope } m = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})^2}, \quad \text{Intercept } b = \bar{y} - m\bar{x}$$

$$\text{Slope per Decade} = 10 \times m$$

$$\text{Goodness of Fit } R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum (y_i - \bar{y})^2}$$

The standard error of the slope $SE(m)$ is computed with $df = n - 2 = 29$ degrees of freedom ($n = 31$ years from 1995 to 2025):
$$SE(m) = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2 / (n - 2)}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}}$$
$$95\% \text{ CI} = [m - 2.045 \times SE(m), \; m + 2.045 \times SE(m)]$$

### 2. Mann-Kendall Non-Parametric Monotonic Trend Test
Evaluates whether a monotonic upward or downward trend exists without assuming normality:
$$S = \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \sum_{j=k+1}^n \text{sgn}(x_j - x_k)$$
where:
$$\text{sgn}(\theta) = \begin{cases} +1 & \text{if } \theta > 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } \theta = 0 \\ -1 & \text{if } \theta < 0 \end{cases}$$

Variance adjusted for tied groups $g$ with group sizes $t_p$:
$$\text{Var}(S) = \frac{n(n-1)(2n+5) - \sum_{p=1}^g t_p(t_p-1)(2t_p+5)}{18}$$

Standardized normal test statistic $Z$:
$$Z = \begin{cases} \frac{S - 1}{\sqrt{\text{Var}(S)}} & \text{if } S > 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } S = 0 \\ \frac{S + 1}{\sqrt{\text{Var}(S)}} & \text{if } S < 0 \end{cases}$$

Two-tailed $p$-value calculated via standard normal cumulative distribution function $\Phi(|Z|)$ using high-precision Horner polynomial approximation ($\epsilon < 1.5 \times 10^{-7}$):
$$p = 2 \times (1 - \Phi(|Z|))$$

### 3. Composite Health Need Index (HNI)
Quantifies localized climate-health urgency on a 0–100 scale:
$$\text{HNI} = 0.35 \times \text{EPI} + 0.30 \times \text{PPI} + 0.20 \times \text{FSI} + 0.15 \times \text{WNI}$$

- **EPI (Environmental Pressure Index):** Composite of normalized $T_{2M\_MAX}$, $PM_{2.5}$, $LST$, and inverted $NDVI$.
- **PPI (Population Pressure Index):** Composite of normalized Population Density and demographic vulnerability.
- **FSI (Facility Shortage Index):** $100 - \text{Normalized Hospital Capacity per 100k}$.
- **WNI (Physician Workload & Need Index):** $0.5 \times (100 - \text{Physicians per 10k normalized}) + 0.5 \times \text{Doctor Field Severity Score}$.

## 🚀 Quickstart & Usage

### 1. Launch the Vite + React Application
The root `index.html` is the Vite **entry template** — it loads `/src/main.jsx` as an ES module, so opening it with a double-click renders a blank page. Serve it instead:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/muhammadsaid16/Terramed-Pulse.git
cd Terramed-Pulse

# Install dependencies (if not already installed)
npm install

# Start lightning-fast Vite development server with HMR
npm run dev

# Or build and preview the optimized production bundle
npm run build
npm run preview
```

> **What needs a network, and what does not.** The dataset, the statistical engine,
> Leaflet and its CSS, the governorate polygons, choropleth and every Chart.js chart are
> bundled — they work with the cable unplugged. Three things are fetched at runtime:
> the **Esri ArcGIS `World_Dark_Gray_Base` tile layer** (offline, the map keeps its polygons,
> markers, legend and time-lapse on a plain grid canvas and shows a "basemap unavailable"
> notice instead of retrying), the **Inter/Cairo webfonts** from Google Fonts (system-font
> fallback), and **the report submission endpoint** when one is deployed. Terramed Pulse is
> offline-*capable*, not fully offline — and since the cloud write path landed, "no
> backend" is true of the data and false of the feed.
>
> **Cloud submission is optional and off by default.** With no endpoint deployed the app
> runs in local-only mode on `localStorage`; nothing requests the network beyond tiles and
> fonts, and the UI says so. The write path is: browser → `POST /api/reports` → one
> Cloudflare Pages Function → Neon Postgres. Set it up in two steps, described in
> `.env.example`: apply `db/migrations/0001_neon_physician_reports.sql` in the Neon SQL
> Editor (the same SQL is copyable from the in-app *Report Submission Endpoint* dialog),
> then attach `NEON_URL` and `IP_SALT` as **Pages secrets**. The browser is given neither.
>
> **What that posture actually allows.** The migration creates an `INSERT`-only role
> called `web_writer`, turns Row Level Security on, and defines the one statement the
> function may issue. `web_writer` can add a validated row and nothing else: no `SELECT`,
> `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `TRUNCATE`; it cannot read the quota table it is rate-limited by, and
> a real login test confirmed every one of those refusals. There is no read endpoint at
> all, so nobody — including this app — can enumerate other physicians' submissions. The
> feed consequently shows this device's own reports — nothing else, and nothing invented —
> and the badge reads
> "cloud submits allowed, reads blocked", which is the intended posture rather than a
> failure. A shared cross-device feed needs authentication plus a read policy; that is a
> disclosure decision about migration intent, not a config flag.
>
> A connection string is not a browser key. An `anon` JWT could be published; the Neon URL
> is owner-level access to every row, so it must never be a `VITE_*` variable — Vite inlines
> every one of those into the public bundle — and never in `.dev.vars`-adjacent files that
> get committed (`.dev.vars` itself is gitignored).

### 2. Run the Full Verification Suite
`npm test` runs all six suites — 474 checks in total: the live React engine (144), the dataset drift gate (22), the submission-contract gate (165), the legacy JS engine (51), and both Python suites (12 and 80):
```bash
cd Terramed-Pulse
npm test
```
Individual suites:
```bash
node src/lib/trendEngine.test.mjs            # 144 checks — the engine the app actually imports
node src/data/terrapulseData.parity.test.mjs #  22 checks — src/data vs legacy/data.json drift gate
node src/lib/syncContract.test.mjs           # 165 checks — SQL policy ↔ validator ↔ form ↔ function
node pipeline/test-legacy-engine.js          #  51 checks — legacy/trend-engine.js parity
python3 pipeline/test_trend_engine.py        #  12 checks — Python statistical engine
python3 pipeline/test_nasa_pipeline.py       #  80 checks — dataset artifacts & cross-runtime parity
```

> **Why the submission-contract gate exists.** The same row shape is written down four
> times, in three languages, in places that are deployed separately: the `CHECK`s and the
> RLS policy in `db/migrations/*.sql` (applied by hand in Neon), the validator in
> `src/lib/reportLimits.js` (run by both the browser and the Pages Function), the caps bound
> to the form's inputs, and the statement issued in `functions/api/reports.js`. Nothing
> notices when they drift; an out-of-date list 400s a legitimate report, and a validator one
> character looser than the table turns a 400 into a database error the UI has to describe as
> a server fault. The gate asserts set equality on identifiers and numeric equality on caps
> across all four, refuses each known poison payload locally, and pins the posture facts that
> were measured against a live database rather than assumed (no `RETURNING`, one statement, no
> read path, no credential in source).

> **Why the drift gate exists.** `pipeline/fetch_nasa_data.py` regenerates `legacy/data.json`
> only; `src/data/terrapulseData.js` is maintained by hand alongside it. The gate asserts the
> two copies are value-for-value identical **except** governorate `lat`/`lng`, which were
> deliberately nudged to polygon centroids for marker placement — and it fails if any other
> field, or any coordinate by more than a degree, diverges.

### 3. Run the NASA POWER Python Data Pipeline
The `pipeline/fetch_nasa_data.py` script queries the real NASA POWER API, computes statistical trends, and exports `data.json`, `data.js` and `embedded-data.js` into `legacy/` (the default `--out-dir`):
```bash
cd Terramed-Pulse

# Fetch live NASA POWER observations into legacy/
python3 pipeline/fetch_nasa_data.py

# Or regenerate from the bundled calibrated cache, with no network
python3 pipeline/fetch_nasa_data.py --offline
```

> **Note:** this pipeline refreshes the **legacy** dataset only. The React app reads
> `src/data/terrapulseData.js`, a separately maintained copy — re-running the pipeline does
> **not** update the application. That split is deliberate (the React file carries hand-nudged
> marker centroids), and the drift gate in suite 2 above is what keeps it honest.
