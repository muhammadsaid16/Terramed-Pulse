Mathematical & Statistical Methodology Guide

1. Linear Regression & Student-t 95% Confidence Interval

Calculates the rate of change (m) per year and per decade (10 × m). Standard error of slope SE(m) is computed from residual variance with df = n - 2 (df = 29 for 31 years), using Student t-critical = 2.045 for two-tailed 95% confidence intervals.

m = Σ(x - x̄)(y - ȳ) / Σ(x - x̄)² | SE(m) = √[SS_res / (df · Σ(x - x̄)²)]

2. Mann-Kendall Non-Parametric Trend Test

Evaluates monotonic upward or downward trends without assuming normal distribution. Computes Mann-Kendall S statistic, variance Var(S) with tie adjustments, standardized Z score, and exact two-tailed p-value via normal CDF.

S = Σ Σ sgn(x_j - x_k) | Z = (S - 1) / √Var(S) | p = 2 · (1 - Φ(|Z|))

3. Composite Health Need Index (HNI)

HNI = 0.35 × EPI + 0.30 × PPI + 0.20 × FSI + 0.15 × WNI, where EPI is Environmental Pressure, PPI is Population Pressure, FSI is Facility Shortage, and WNI is Physician Workload & Shortage.
